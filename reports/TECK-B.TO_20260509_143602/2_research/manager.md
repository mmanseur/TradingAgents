### Research Manager’s Critical Evaluation & Investment Plan

**Instrument:** `TECK-B.TO`  
**Current Price:** CAD 88.75  
**Debate Summary:**  
The bull argues for continued buying based on Teck’s transformation to a pure‑play copper producer, record Q1 earnings (EBITDA +125%, EPS +207%), a strong balance sheet, and a technical re‑acceleration. The bear counters that these extraordinary results are already fully priced – the stock failed to rally on the news, is technically overextended, faces macro headwinds (strong USD, Freeport’s production cuts highlighting sector risk), and offers a poor short‑term risk/reward as consensus targets sit below the current price.

---

### Evaluation of Key Arguments

- **Earnings Reaction:** The most telling data point is the stock’s flat performance (*–0.4%* on the week of record results). In a healthy uptrend, such blowout numbers should attract fresh buying. A sideways drift—while the broader copper sector fell 5.5%—suggests selling pressure absorbed the good news. This “buy the rumor, sell the fact” pattern is a classic warning that the near‑term upside is exhausted.

- **Currency & Macro:** The bull’s translation‑tailwind argument is partial. A strong USD simultaneously pressures USD‑denominated copper prices (as flagged by RBC) and hurts marginal global demand. The net effect for a Canadian miner is ambiguous at best, and the bear correctly notes that elevated copper prices are the lifeblood of the bull thesis. Freeport’s operational stumbles, while supportive of copper prices long‑term, inject a risk premium that makes it harder to justify Teck’s already stretched multiple.

- **Valuation & Growth Sustainability:** The trailing P/E of 23.5x and a PEG of 5.44 signal that the market is paying for perfection. The bull’s forward P/E of ~14‑15x assumes earnings will persist at peak levels and that QB2’s volume growth is a durable compounder rather than a one‑time step‑change. History shows that mining earnings are cyclical; paying a growth multiple for a non‑recurring ramp‑up is risky, especially when the stock did not respond to its best quarter ever.

- **Technical Setup:** The bear’s Bollinger Band argument holds weight. The stock is above the upper band—a condition that has preceded two corrections of 23‑26% in the past year. The RSI at 66.57 is not yet overbought, but the MACD crossover is a lagging signal that can trap bulls into a false re‑acceleration. Elevated volume on flat days near an all‑time high often points to distribution, not accumulation.

- **Analyst Consensus & Sentiment:** The consensus analyst target is CAD 80, materially below the current price. Mainstream outlets asking “Is it too late to buy?” reflect FOMO sentiment that historically marks a local peak, not a “wall of worry” when the story is already well‑known.

**Conclusion:** The bear’s final risk/reward calculation is the most objective piece of math in the debate. Using the bull’s own upside target of CAD 100 and the nearest technical support (50‑day SMA at ~CAD 75), the ratio is approximately 0.82:1 – asymmetric **downside**. The bull case relies on everything continuing perfectly; the bear case is grounded in observable price exhaustion, an unfavorable short‑term valuation, and elevated technical risk.

---

### Final Rating: **Underweight**

The fundamental story is compelling long‑term, but the current entry point is aggressive and the market is sending clear signals of near‑term exhaustion. A cautious stance is warranted: trim positions to lock in gains and reduce exposure while waiting for a more attractive re‑entry.

---

### Actionable Investment Plan for TECK-B.TO

1. **Reduce Position Size**  
   - Sell **30‑50%** of any existing holdings at current levels (CAD 88.75) or into any minor strength toward CAD 90.  
   - This preserves the core exposure if the multi‑year thesis plays out, but materially lowers portfolio risk.

2. **Set a Hard Stop‑Loss on Remaining Position**  
   - Place a stop‑loss at **CAD 82.00** (just below the recent consolidation low and the 20‑day EMA).  
   - If triggered, exit the remainder. This caps maximum downside from current price to roughly 7.5%, well within the 15‑22% historical correction range.

3. **Identify Re‑Entry Levels**  
   - **Re‑accumulate** only on a pullback to the **CAD 73‑78 zone**, which aligns with:  
     - The rising 50‑day SMA (~CAD 75)  
     - The bottom of prior correction ranges  
     - A forward P/E that would re‑rate closer to the sector average.  
   - Wait for a confirmed bullish technical reversal (e.g., RSI reset below 50, MACD turn from oversold) before adding.

4. **Monitor Catalysts**  
   - Q2 2026 results (late July): If the stock again fails to rally on strong numbers, that would confirm distribution and warrant a full exit even from the remaining core.  
   - Copper price developments and USD strength: A sustained break below USD 4.20/lb copper or a further aggressive hawkish Fed stance would invalidate the bull’s earnings assumptions—use that as a trigger to sell the residual position entirely.

5. **Dividend & Buyback Context**  
   - The negligible dividend (0.56%) offers no income cushion. The buyback is positive but already factored into earnings‑per‑share estimates; it does not justify an unhedged long at this extended price.

---

**Summary:**  
`TECK-B.TO` is an excellent company in a structural growth sector, but that does not automatically make the stock a buy at any price. The short‑term risk/reward is unfavorable, the tape is showing exhaustion, and a disciplined trim de‑risks the trade while keeping the long‑term story on the radar. **Underweight** is the prudent rating.
